ECS

#ecs #beginner

Bevy is built on an Entity Component System (ECS). Data lives in components, which are attached to entities; logic lives in systems.

Entities and components

An entity is just an ID. You give it meaning by inserting components. Step through the systems one at a time, or press play: spawn_player runs once at startup, then regenerate heals the player every frame.

Note
Since Bevy 0.20, resources are components stored on singleton entities, so most of the APIs below work for both.

Systems

Systems are plain Rust functions whose parameters describe the data they need. Bevy runs systems in parallel when their data access does not conflict. They are also how scenes attach behaviour with on(...).

Queries

QueryMeaning
Query<&T>Read T on every entity that has it
Query<&mut T>Write T
Query<&T, With<U>>Only entities that also have U
Query<Entity, Changed<T>>Entities whose T changed since the system last ran
