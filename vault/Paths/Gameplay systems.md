# Gameplay systems

The pieces most games need, each small enough to build in an afternoon.

1. [[Gameplay]]: the map of this area.
2. [[Game AI]]: a guard that patrols and chases.
3. [[Pathfinding]]: get around walls.
4. [[Randomness]]: seeds and fair luck.
5. [[Procedural generation]]: levels from rules.
6. [[Inventory and items]]: carry things.
7. [[Dialogue]] (optional): talk to people.
8. [[Game feel]] (optional): make hits land.
9. [[Accessibility and localization]] (optional): more players, more languages.
10. [[bevy_rand]] (extra): random numbers the ECS way.
