# Your first game

The main quest: from an empty window to **Dodge**, a small game you build yourself. On the map it traces Bevy's bird, with [[Bevy]] as its eye. Every other route branches off it.

1. [[Start]]: where everyone begins.
2. [[Hello Bevy]]: a window and a spinning box.

6. [[Entities and components]]: describe things.
7. [[Systems]]: change them.
8. [[Input]] (optional): keys and the mouse.
9. [[Queries]]: find what to change.
10. [[Transforms]]: move, turn and scale.
11. [[Sprites]]: pictures on screen.
12. [[Commands]]: spawn and despawn.
13. [[Resources]]: one value for the whole game, like the score.
14. [[Time and timers]]: do it every so often.
15. [[Events and observers]]: react to a click.
16. [[Run conditions]]: freeze the game when it is over.
17. [[Make a game]]: put it all together into Dodge.
18. [[Audio]] (optional): a sound when you get hit.
19. [[Game feel]] (optional): make hits land.
